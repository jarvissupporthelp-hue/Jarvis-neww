# Day 24 UFAR pilot

**Status:** Protocol prepared. Independent participant sessions and acceptance results are pending.

## Before the session

- Recruit 3–5 students and one lecturer. Assign participant codes S1–S5 and L1.
- Use the [staging account page](https://jarvis-git-day-twenty-three-usability-jarvis-8637.vercel.app/account) and synthetic course data. Give each participant a separate staging login privately. Confirm they can reach the protected preview and that their accounts have the correct roles and course assignments.
- Check that the students have an upcoming class and an enrolled pilot course. Check that the lecturer is assigned to that course. Prepare a short sample assignment brief and a future deadline in Yerevan time.
- Ask participants to use their own devices. Record device, browser, and viewport where available. Have them work independently and record their first attempts before giving help.

## Participant task cards

| Role | Task to give the participant | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Find your next class. Tell us the course, start time, and room. | Details match the staged timetable. Record where the participant found them. |
| Student | Create and save a study plan for an exam in an enrolled course. Use a future exam date and add at least one topic. Reload and find the plan you saved. | The correct course and date appear in a saved plan after reload. A saved draft satisfies this task. |
| Lecturer | Publish the sample assignment brief with the supplied deadline for your assigned course. | The published brief has the correct course and Yerevan deadline. An observer verifies it in an enrolled student's dashboard and calendar. |

Give participants the task text and sample inputs only. If they need help, record the blocker before assisting. Record any later successful attempt separately.

## Acceptance

Close Day 24 after 3–5 students independently find their next class and save a plan, and one lecturer independently publishes a deadline. Report independent success counts for each task and any blockers, including assisted attempts. After recording the results, disable temporary pilot accounts and retire synthetic records as appropriate.
